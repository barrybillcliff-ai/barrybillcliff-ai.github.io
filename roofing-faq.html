How do I know if I need roof repair or full replacement?

Look at the roof age, the pattern of the leaks, shingle brittleness, missing tabs, flashing failures, and whether the same section keeps being patched. A good roof check should explain whether a repair still buys meaningful time or whether replacement is the smarter long-term choice.

What should I do if my roof is actively leaking?

Protect the inside first. Move furniture and valuables, contain the drip if possible, and keep people away from wet ceiling areas. Do not climb on a wet, icy, steep, or visibly damaged roof. Photos of the attic, ceiling stain, or missing shingles can help the first call move faster.

Can a leak come from flashing, valleys, or gutters instead of shingles?

Yes. Roof leaks often come from chimneys, skylights, wall intersections, valleys, pipe boots, step flashing, ridge details, gutters, or roof edges. That is why the right inspection looks at the full roof system instead of blaming the shingles automatically.

Do gutters and ventilation really affect roof life?

They do. Overflowing gutters, poor drainage, weak attic airflow, and ignored roof-edge details can shorten roof life and create repeat problems that look like shingle failure from the ground.

Can Modern Vision Roofing help with flat and low-slope roofs?

Yes. The site is positioned around EPDM and TPO conversations, low-slope leak troubleshooting, drainage review, seam concerns, penetrations, and replacement planning for commercial and mixed-use properties.

What should be in a roofing estimate?

A useful estimate should cover the repair or replacement scope, material direction, flashing details, ventilation, cleanup expectations, and what the next step is. Homeowners deserve more than a single number with no explanation behind it.
