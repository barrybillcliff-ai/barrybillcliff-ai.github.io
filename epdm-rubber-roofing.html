When property owners call about this

  • Open or stressed seams and patches
  • Membrane shrinkage at walls, curbs, drains, or penetrations
  • Punctures, surface damage, or movement at roof edges
  • Ponding water and recurring leaks that travel before appearing indoors

How the repair-versus-replacement decision should be made

EPDM can be a practical long-life low-slope material when the system is designed and installed around the actual roof geometry. Repairability depends on membrane age and condition, contamination, adhesion, wet insulation, seam condition, and whether drainage problems are causing repeated stress.

Safety and scope limits

A leak location indoors may not match the entry point on a low-slope roof. Avoid assumptions based only on the ceiling stain; tracing water paths and checking the surrounding system matters.

Common questions

What should I share when I call?

Share the property address, roof type if known, where the problem is visible, when it started, prior repair history, and safe photos. Those details help organize the first conversation.

Does every problem require full replacement?

No. The recommendation should follow the extent of damage, surrounding material condition, roof age, repair history, drainage, flashing, and whether the problem is isolated or system-wide.
