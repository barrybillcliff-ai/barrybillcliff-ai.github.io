Call for roof repair

Common roof repair calls

  • Active or intermittent roof leaks
  • Missing, lifted, cracked, or storm-damaged shingles
  • Pipe boot, chimney, skylight, valley, and flashing problems
  • Roof-edge, fascia, and gutter-related water intrusion
  • Snow, wind, and storm wear that spreads if it is ignored

Repair or replace?

A strong roof evaluation should explain both paths. If a repair buys real time, say so. If replacement is the more responsible long-term move, explain why clearly instead of guessing.
