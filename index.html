How do I know if I should repair or replace the roof?

Look at the age of the roof, how many leak points exist, whether shingles are brittle or lifting, and whether the same areas keep needing patches. A good inspection should explain both paths clearly.

What should I do during an active roof leak?

Protect the interior, move valuables, contain the water if possible, and avoid climbing on the roof. Photos of the ceiling, attic, or exterior damage can help speed up the first call.

Do gutters and ventilation really affect roof life?

Yes. Roof problems often trace back to drainage, attic airflow, flashing, or roof-edge details, not just the visible shingles.

Can Modern Vision Roofing help with flat roofs too?

Yes. The site is set up for EPDM and TPO conversations, seam and drainage reviews, and low-slope repair or replacement planning.
