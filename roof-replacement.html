Replacement roofing

Roof replacement options that balance cost, protection, and curb appeal.

When replacement makes sense

A full replacement may be the right move when the roof is older, has repeated leaks, shows curling or missing shingles, has damaged decking, or no longer protects the building reliably.

  • Architectural asphalt shingle replacement
  • Storm or wind-damaged roof replacement planning
  • Underlayment, flashing, ventilation, and drip-edge review
  • Gutter and roofline concerns checked at the same time

Clear estimate approach

The goal is to give property owners a clear scope, explain the material and budget tradeoffs, and avoid overselling work that is not needed.
